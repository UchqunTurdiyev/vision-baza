# Performance Marketing — Xalqaro manbalar va darsliklar

Vision Group "Performance Marketing va Target kursi" uchun rasmiy va ishonchli manbalar to'plami. Quyidagilar Meta, Google va jahon miqyosidagi mutaxassislarning bepul/rasmiy resurslari.

---

## 1. Meta (Facebook / Instagram) — rasmiy

**Meta Blueprint** — Meta'ning rasmiy o'quv platformasi. 100+ bepul kurs (boshlang'ichdan ilg'orgacha). O'quv materiallari bepul, sertifikat imtihoni pullik.

- Meta Blueprint asosiy sahifa: https://www.facebook.com/business/learn
- Meta Certified Performance Marketing Specialist (rasmiy yo'nalish): https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/page/582867-become-a-meta-certified-performance-marketing-specialist
- Coursera'dagi rasmiy "Meta Social Media Marketing Professional Certificate": https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/facebook-social-media-marketing

**Sertifikatlar tuzilishi:**
- 100-101 Meta Certified Digital Marketing Associate — ~$99
- 410-101 Meta Certified Media Buying Professional — ~$150 (Performance Marketing Specialist nishoniga yo'l ochadi)

---

## 2. Google — rasmiy

**Google Skillshop** — Google'ning rasmiy bepul o'quv platformasi (eski nomi Google Academy for Ads). Barcha kurs va sertifikatlar bepul.

- Skillshop asosiy sahifa: https://skillshop.withgoogle.com/
- AI-Powered Performance Ads Certification (eng dolzarb): https://www.classcentral.com/course/skillshop-ai-powered-performance-ads-certification-494099
- Google Ads sertifikatlari (Search, Display, Video, Shopping, App): https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7539883

**Imtihon formati:** 40–75 ta savol, 75 daqiqa, o'tish bali 80%, qayta topshirish cheksiz. Sertifikat 12 oydan keyin yangilanadi.

---

## 3. HubSpot Academy — bepul sertifikatlar

Inbound va raqamli marketing bo'yicha jahon yetakchisi. Ro'yxatdan o'tish 100% bepul.

- HubSpot Academy kurslari: https://academy.hubspot.com/courses
- Raqamli marketing sertifikati (universitetlar uchun syllabus ham bor): https://academy.hubspot.com/education-partner-program/inbound-digital-marketing-syllabus-for-professors

> Maslahat: HubSpot'ning syllabusi tayyor kurs tuzilishi sifatida juda foydali — o'z kursingiz dasturini shu asosda solishtirib chiqishingiz mumkin.

---

## 4. Jahon mutaxassislari — maqolalar va bloglar

**Neil Patel** — dunyodagi eng mashhur raqamli marketing ekspertlaridan biri (Crazy Egg, KISSmetrics asoschisi). SEO, trafik, konversiya bo'yicha amaliy maqolalar.
- Blog: https://neilpatel.com/blog/

**HubSpot Marketing Blog** — oyiga 4.5M+ o'quvchi. Kampaniyalar qurish, ROI, ma'lumotlarga asoslangan marketing.
- Blog: https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing

## Kursni qurish uchun tavsiya

1. **Asosiy skelet** — Meta Blueprint + Google Skillshop yo'nalishlarini modul sifatida oling (Meta Ads, Google Ads, o'lchov/measurement, AI-avtomatlashtirish).
2. **Nazariy mustahkamlash** — HubSpot Academy syllabusidan inbound/voronka qismini moslang.
3. **Amaliy kontent** — Neil Patel va HubSpot bloglaridan keys va misollar.
4. **Sertifikat qiymati** — talabalarga Meta/Google rasmiy bepul sertifikatlarini olishni ham vazifa qilib bering — bu kursingiz qiymatini oshiradi.
